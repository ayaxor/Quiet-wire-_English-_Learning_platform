

🇬🇧 Level 5 — Advanced

📌 Overview

This level prepares you to communicate exactly like professionals in companies.
You’ll learn how to speak clearly, handle meetings, express ideas, and discuss technical topics (cybersecurity, AI, projects…).


---

🎯 Who is this for?

Learners preparing for important meetings

Students who want professional-level English

Anyone aiming to speak like a native in work situations

Trainees in cybersecurity / AI programs



---

🧠 What you will learn

Advanced communication skills

Professional expressions for meetings

Handling questions / giving explanations

Talking about technical projects

Structuring long answers confidently

Strong vocabulary for work & training



---

🔥 Professional English Phrases (Advanced)

🟦 Explaining your work

I’m currently working on improving my skills in cybersecurity and GitHub.

I completed all the required repositories under QuietWire training.

I’m preparing myself for the upcoming meeting.


🟦 Giving strong answers

Based on my understanding…

From my perspective…

My main priority right now is…

I would like to clarify that…

I strongly believe that…


🟦 Handling difficult questions

That’s a great question. Let me explain.

I need one moment to think about it.

Here is the main idea…

As far as I know…

I will double-check and update you.
