🌟 Level 2 — Elementary

English Learning Program (QuietWire Edition)

📌 Overview

This level builds on the basics.
It focuses on short sentences, real-life expressions, and simple conversations.

🎯 Who is this for?

Learners who know basic words

Students preparing to communicate in simple situations

Anyone improving fluency step-by-step


🧠 What you learn

Daily sentences

Asking & answering questions

Talking about routines

Useful verbs and common vocabulary


🗣️ Example Sentences

I wake up at 6 a.m.

I go to school every day.

I am learning English for my future.

I can speak a little English.
