🇬🇧 Level 4 — Intermediate

📌 Overview

This level helps you start speaking naturally and professionally.
You learn to express opinions, describe tasks, and talk confidently in meetings.

🎯 Who is this for?

Learners who can make sentences and want to speak fluently

Students preparing for interviews & meetings

Anyone who wants stronger communication in work & study



---

🧠 What you will learn

Story-based reading

Clear paragraph writing

Strong vocabulary for meetings

How to describe tasks, goals, challenges

How to talk about yourself confidently

Improving pronunciation & speaking flow



---

🗣️ Important Speaking Patterns

Use these in your daily conversation 👇

🔹 Talking about progress

I made good progress this week.

I improved my English and my confidence.

I learned new vocabulary and practiced speaking.


🔹 Talking about work/tasks

I completed my task on GitHub.

I am working on a new assignment now.

I organized my schedule and my priorities.


🔹 Talking about goals

My goal is to speak English fluently.

I want to communicate clearly in meetings.

I aim to improve my cybersecurity knowledge.

💬 Mini Conversation (Professional)

Manager: Can you give me a quick update?
Aya: Yes, I improved my English, finished my tasks, and prepared for the meeting.
Manager: Excellent. What is your next goal?
Aya: I want to speak more fluently and handle technical discussions.


---

📘 Mini Reading Exercise

> Aya is now speaking more confidently.
She practices English every day and uses it in her cybersecurity training.
Her goal is to communicate like a professional and impress her team.



Questions:

1. How is Aya speaking now?


2. Why is she practicing English?


3. What is her goal?
