🇬🇧 Level 3 — Pre-Intermediate

📌 Overview

This level helps you move from basic English to real communication.
You start forming clear sentences, reading paragraphs, and understanding conversations.

🎯 Who is this for?

Learners who can make simple sentences

Students preparing for meetings

Anyone who wants stronger speaking & understanding


🧠 What you will learn

Paragraph reading

Clear sentence building

Describing your work, tasks, skills

Speaking with confidence

Asking and answering longer questions



---

🗣️ Useful sentences

I am improving my English every day.

I can explain my ideas more clearly now.

I am preparing for an important meeting.

I want to communicate professionally.

I am learning cybersecurity and English at the same time.

💬 Mini Conversation (Meeting Style)

A: Can you explain your progress this week?
B: Yes, I improved my English, organized my tasks, and worked on GitHub.
A: Great. Are you ready for the next step?
B: Yes, I feel more confident now.


---

📘 Mini Reading Exercise

Read this short paragraph:

> Aya is preparing for an important meeting. She is learning English every day and practicing speaking. She wants to communicate clearly and confidently.



Questions:

1. What is Aya preparing for?


2. Why is she learning English?


3. How does she want to speak?
